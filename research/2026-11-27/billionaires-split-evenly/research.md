# Split every billionaire's fortune evenly — what check does each person on Earth get?
What If · "Everyone argues about it" math · compiled 2026-11-26 for Fri 2026-11-27 · computed in Python · NEUTRAL (no opinion on wealth)

## The claim in one line
Forbes counts **3,428 billionaires worth $20.1 trillion** (March 2026). Divide it by everyone alive (about 8.2 billion) and each person gets about **$2,451, once**. It sounds like a lot of money in one place; split evenly it is a one-time check that the average billionaire's wealth would refill in a blink.

## Assumptions (on screen: "ASSUMES")
- Forbes' World's Billionaires list, **March 2026** (stock prices and exchange rates as of Mar 1, 2026): **3,428** billionaires, **$20.1 trillion** combined.
- World population **about 8.2 billion** (UN-type estimate; rounded; not freshly opened).
- Hypothetical: assumes every dollar of that paper wealth could be turned into cash with no price drop (it can't — most is company stock) and handed out **once**.
- No opinion about whether this is good or bad.

## Formula
$20.1 trillion ÷ 8.2 billion = **$2,451.22** per person.
