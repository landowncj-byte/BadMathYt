# Anna Sorokin ("Anna Delvey") — "She told New York she had $67 million. Every card she handed over was declined."

## Story beats
1. A young woman in New York's art/party world calls herself **"Anna Delvey"**, a German heiress with a **trust fund of about $67 million** overseas (NBC). Real name: Anna Sorokin, from Russia, raised in Germany.
2. She stays in the nicest hotels, eats at the best restaurants, wears designer clothes — and wants a bank loan for an arts club (loan details ⚠ — skip the $22M).
3. The pattern: she never pays. A card "has an issue" and someone else covers it; the money "is coming by wire".
4. **2017:** two luxury hotels — the **Beekman** and the **W** — report she left without paying (Beekman tab about **$11,500**). At a restaurant one night **all 12** of her card numbers were declined (concierge's account, A&E/Oxygen retelling ✔1).
5. A friend, Vanity Fair photo editor **Rachel DeLoache Williams**, ends up covering a **$62,000** Morocco trip bill when Delvey says the wire is coming. The wire never comes.
6. **Oct 2017:** arrested. Williams later helped (sting) and wrote the Vanity Fair story (2018).
7. **April 25, 2019:** a Manhattan jury convicts on **8 of 10 counts** (grand larceny + theft of services). **May 9/10, 2019: sentenced to 4 to 12 years**, fined $24,000, ordered to repay about $199,000.
8. Payoff: the tell was never the clothes, the accent, or the story. It was the **money that never arrived** — the card that failed, the wire that never landed.
