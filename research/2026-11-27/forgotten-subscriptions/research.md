## The claim in one line

## Assumptions (on screen: "ASSUMES")
- $10 per month each (round number; real prices vary — this is not an average and no brand is meant).
- Six subscriptions you stopped using but never canceled. 12 months a year, price never rises. No interest or investing.
- Hypothetical example, not a survey. (Do NOT claim "the average American has six forgotten subscriptions" — not sourced.)

## Formula
Cost = count × price × 12 × years.
