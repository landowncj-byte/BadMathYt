# Han van Meegeren — "He sold a Nazi leader a fake masterpiece. To prove it, he had to paint another one in front of the judges."
Decoded · Fake-art con with a twist (the forger needed to prove he was a forger) · compiled 2026-11-26 for Fri 2026-11-27

**Access note:** Wikipedia/Britannica fetches blocked; read via WebSearch extracts. ✔2 = two outlets · ✔1 · ⚠ conflict.

## Story beats
1. 1930s–40s, Netherlands: Han van Meegeren, a painter critics ignored, secretly paints "lost" works in the style of **Johannes Vermeer** (17th-century Dutch master; there are only about three dozen known Vermeers — "about three dozen" ✔ general knowledge; Rijksmuseum count ⚠ not opened).
2. He sells them as newly discovered Vermeers; experts and museums buy.
3. **1943:** during the Nazi occupation one, *Christ with the Adulteress*, is sold through a dealer to **Hermann Göring** for **1.65 million guilders** (Dutch WWII museum site ✔1 + Essential Vermeer ✔1). 
4. **1945:** Allied troops find it in Göring's collection and trace it to van Meegeren. Selling a Dutch national treasure to the enemy: he's arrested and faces charges of **collaboration** — a possible death-penalty/long-prison charge.
5. The tell flips: he says **"I didn't sell a treasure. I painted it."** Nobody believes him — so he **paints a new "Vermeer" while officials watch**, using the same methods (Britannica: under observation; 1947 newspaper: a "Christ among the elders" painting).
6. **Trial Oct 1947;** prosecutor asks 2 years; **Nov 12, 1947: convicted of forgery/fraud, sentenced to 1 year** (collaboration charge dropped).
7. He dies of a heart attack within weeks, before serving it (date ⚠ Nov 26 vs Dec 30 → "weeks after the verdict" / "before the year ended").
8. Payoff: in the Netherlands he'd become a folk hero — the man who tricked Göring. The con that should have hanged him was the thing that saved him.

## Story loop
STAKES: sell a Nazi chief a fake and the penalty could be death. · QUESTION: how do you get out of that? · TWIST: confess to the fraud — and prove it with a paintbrush. · REHOOKS: "1.65 million guilders" → "arrested as a traitor" → "he asked for a canvas" → "one year".

## Convictions
- **Han van Meegeren** — convicted of forgery and fraud, Amsterdam, 12 Nov 1947, 1 year in prison (never served; he died). "Forger", "convicted", "sentenced to one year" are safe.
- Göring was a Nazi leader convicted at Nuremberg (1946) — don't go there; unrelated.
