# Wells Fargo — "A bank opened millions of accounts nobody asked for."
Decoded · Corporate / everyday banking (everyone has a bank account) · compiled 2026-11-26 for Sat 2026-12-05

**Access note:** WebSearch extracts only; DOJ/CFPB pages appeared in results. Grades ✔2/✔1/⚠. Corporate outcome = DOJ deferred-prosecution + penalties (company admitted facts); one executive pleaded guilty. Say "bank admitted", not "bank was convicted".

## Story beats
1. **The goal:** the bank pushed a "cross-selling" strategy — sell each customer **more products** (checking, savings, credit card). Its public "cross-sale ratio" was about **six** products per customer (CFPB-era extract ✔1).
2. **Pressure:** employees faced **unrealistic sales goals** (DOJ statement of facts ✔2: 2002–2016). Managers set quotas; people were threatened or fired for missing them (✔1 → "prosecutors say there was pressure").
3. **The con:** employees **opened accounts customers never asked for**, used **fake PINs, forged signatures, moved money between accounts ("simulated funding")** to hit numbers (✔2 DOJ press release + coverage).
4. **Scale:** the bank's own analysis showed **more than 2 million** possibly-unauthorized deposit and credit-card accounts (about **1.5M deposit + 565K credit cards**) (✔2: CFPB 2016 / Fortune; the later "3.5 million" figure is **⚠ not confirmed** → don't voice).
5. **Sept 2016:** regulators fine the bank **$185 million** ($100M CFPB, $35M OCC, $50M Los Angeles); about **5,300 employees fired** over the conduct (✔2: NPR/CBS/KQED).
6. **Feb 2020:** the bank agrees to pay **$3 billion** to resolve DOJ criminal and SEC civil probes — **$2.5B DOJ, $0.5B SEC**; a three-year deferred prosecution agreement; admits top managers knew about the practices (✔2: DOJ, NPR, PBS).
7. **Individuals:** ex-head of community banking **Carrie Tolstedt** pleaded guilty (2023) to obstructing a bank examination; **Sept 2023 sentenced to 3 years probation (incl. 6 months home confinement)** and **$17 million** civil penalty to OCC; plus $4.9M SEC settlement (✔2: Fortune/CourtHouse/Compliance Week/KRDO). CEO John Stumpf received a lifetime banking ban (✔1 Compliance Week extract).
8. Payoff/tell: the "tell" was in the numbers — customers with accounts they never opened, cards they never asked for, and the same employees opening hundreds.

## Story loop
STAKES: you trust your bank not to invent accounts in your name. · QUESTION: why would anyone open a fake account? · TWIST: because their job and bonus depended on selling "eight" products to each customer. · REHOOKS: "quotas" → "fake PIN" → "5,300 fired" → "$3 billion" → "probation".

## Convictions
Corporate: DPA + admitted facts ($3B). Individual: **Carrie Tolstedt — guilty plea, obstruction of a bank examination; probation**. No others convicted in the extracts.

## Don't say
- **"3.5 million" fake accounts.** "Eight is great" / "Gr8" slogan — not found in extracts; skip unless re-sourced.
- That anyone went to prison. Say "the executive got probation".
- That all fired employees were guilty — many were low-level; the DOJ blamed top-down pressure. Roast the bank/quota system, not workers.
- Bank logos/colours (rule 65): use "a big American bank" cards, say the name aloud only.
- Customer harm beyond what the DOJ said (late fees etc. ✔ unseen).
