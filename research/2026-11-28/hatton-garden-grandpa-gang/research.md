# Hatton Garden 2015 — "Seven old guys robbed a vault. Their own cars recorded the confession."
Decoded · Heist (London's "biggest burglary in English legal history") · compiled 2026-11-26 for Thu 2026-12-03

**Access note:** WebSearch extracts only. Grades ✔2/✔1/⚠.

## Story beats
1. **Easter weekend 2015, London.** Hatton Garden is the city's jewellery district; a basement safe-deposit company holds hundreds of private boxes. A gang of mostly **older men (ringleader in his 70s)** gets in via a lift shaft and **drills through a thick concrete vault wall** (✔2: Fox/IBTimes/Guardian-type extracts). Haul **worth more than £14 million** (✔2).
2. The raid is not smooth: two of the men (**Brian Reader and Carl Wood**) reportedly pulled out partway through, fearing capture; others return for a second night against the leader's wishes (✔1 Crime+Investigation/Daily Beast → cut or "reportedly").
3. Police review about **150 CCTV cameras** and spot a car near the building (BuzzFeed ✔1 "about 150"; white Mercedes ✔1).
4. **Mid-May 2015:** detectives place **covert audio devices in the gang's cars** and listen to them argue about what went wrong and how to split the loot (inkl timeline/BuzzFeed ✔2).
5. **19 May 2015:** nine arrests; police raid addresses, recover loot (✔2).
6. **Pleas/verdict:** several pleaded guilty; **Jan 2016** a jury convicted others (NPR "seven British men guilty" ✔2). Judge called it the "biggest burglary in English legal history" (✔2).
7. **Sentences, March 2016:** four got **7 years**, Wood **6 years**; Reader about **6 years 3 months** (ill; appeared by video link); the electronics man Michael Seed got **10 years** later (**2019**) (✔2 for each: Garden Court Chambers, Yahoo/AOL, HuffPost, Fox).
8. Payoff: a gang famous for old-school skill got undone by two things from the modern world: cameras everywhere and a recorded argument in their own cars. Much of the loot was never recovered (⚠ say "much of it never found" only if re-sourced).
