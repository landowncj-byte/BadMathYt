## Story beats
1. 2016: Charlie Javice, in her early 20s, founds **Frank**, a website that helps students fill in the federal aid form (FAFSA). 2019: Forbes 30 Under 30.
2. 2021: she shops the company to big banks. The pitch: Frank has **4.25 million** users (the website said 4.25 million students). In reality it had **fewer than 300,000** (SEC) — prosecutors said "several hundred thousand".
3. JPMorgan does the obvious thing and asks for the customer list to check.
4. THE TELL: Javice first asks Frank's own director of engineering to create "synthetic" data. He declines. (SEC)
5. She and growth chief Olivier Amar then hire an outside **data-science professor**, who generates a list of about 4.265 million fake customer records. Reported price: **$18,000** (JPMorgan's lawsuit).
6. September 2021: JPMorgan buys Frank for **$175 million**. Javice becomes a managing director at the bank.
7. After the deal, the bank tries to market to "its" new customers and the list falls apart. (Widely reported detail: a test email to ~400,000 names mostly bounced — ⚠ not verified this session.)
8. Late 2022: JPMorgan sues; April 4, 2023: DOJ and SEC charge her.
9. March 28, 2025: a Manhattan jury convicts Javice and Amar on all four counts. September 29, 2025: **85 months** in prison.
10. Sting in the tail: because the deal made her a JPMorgan employee, a Delaware court made the bank pay her legal bills — over $100 million; the judge then put the bill into the **$287.5 million** restitution order.

## Convictions
- **Charlie Javice** — convicted by jury 2025-03-28 (SDNY, case 23-cr-251, Judge Hellerstein) of conspiracy, wire fraud, bank fraud, securities fraud. Sentenced 2025-09-29 to **85 months**. A judge rejected a bid to overturn the conviction in March 2026; she reportedly sought a presidential pardon in June 2026 (Wikipedia) — status of any appeal/pardon as of today **not verified**.
- **Olivier Amar** — convicted same day, same counts. Sentenced 2025-11-05 to **68 months**.
- SEC civil case filed 2023-04-04 (outcome not checked).
