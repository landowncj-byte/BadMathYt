# "The $40 Billion Coin That Was Backed by Nothing" — Do Kwon, TerraUSD and Luna

Series: Decoded (long). Not used before (ideas.md has Madoff, Ponzi, Salad Oil, FTX, Theranos, Fyre — no Terra/Luna). Court-proven: **guilty plea 2025-08-12; 15 years, 2025-12-11 (SDNY)**; SEC jury verdict April 2024 + $4.47B settlement.

**Why this story:** one impossible promise (a "dollar" with no dollars behind it paying ~20%), a villain with on-the-record arrogance (his own tweets), a collapse you can show as arithmetic (the death spiral), a manhunt with a fake passport, and a fresh sentence. ## Chapter 1 — The promise: a dollar made of math (≈0:00–2:00)
Beats
1. Cold open on the number: $40 billion gone in a week, and the founder's tweet from ten months earlier: "I don't debate the poor on Twitter."
2. Who: Do Kwon, born 1991, Stanford computer-science graduate (⚠ education from memory — verify), co-founds Terraform Labs in 2018 with Daniel Shin.
3. The product: **TerraUSD (UST)**, a "stablecoin" meant to always be worth $1. Normal stablecoins claim to hold a real dollar per coin. UST held none. It was "algorithmic".
4. The mechanism in one line: you can always swap 1 UST for **$1 worth of Luna** (a sister coin), and back. If UST dips to 98¢, traders buy it and swap for $1 of Luna — profit — pushing it back to $1.
5. The catch (plant it now): that only works while Luna is worth something.
6. The bait: **Anchor Protocol** — deposit UST, earn about **19.5%** a year. By spring 2022 roughly **$14 billion** of the ~$18.7 billion UST in existence was parked there.
7. Bad-math beat: 19.45% on $14B is about **$2.7 billion a year** in interest — about $7.5 million a day — that had to come from somewhere. It came mostly from reserves topped up by Terraform's backers.

## Chapter 2 — The first crack and the first lie (≈2:00–3:45)
Beats
1. **May 2021:** UST slips below $1. Then it recovers. Kwon tells the world the algorithm healed itself — proof the design works.
2. What he later admitted in court: it didn't heal itself. A trading firm had been brought in to buy UST and prop it up, and he lied about why the peg came back. (Plea, Aug 2025.)
3. Second lie: Terraform said a Korean payments app, **Chai**, was running real purchases on the Terra blockchain — proof of real-world use. DOJ: Chai processed payments through ordinary financial networks, not the blockchain.
4. More, per DOJ: he misrepresented who controlled the "independent" reserve fund (the Luna Foundation Guard) and a product called Mirror.
5. After the "self-healing" story, money floods in. Luna runs from under $1 (early 2021) to an all-time high of **$119.51** (April 2022).
6. The persona: June 30/July 1, 2021 — a UK economist explains how the peg could fail; he replies "I don't debate the poor on Twitter." Days before the crash he says "95% [of coins] are going to die, but there's also entertainment in watching companies die too."

## Chapter 3 — Bigger, louder, more fragile (≈3:45–5:15)
Beats
1. Early 2022: Kwon sets up the **Luna Foundation Guard** and buys Bitcoin as a backstop: **80,394 BTC**, about $2.4 billion at the time, by May 7.
2. Do the ratio: ~$2.4B of Bitcoin (plus other assets) behind ~$18.7B of "dollars". About 13 cents of hard backing per UST.
3. Feb 2022: Terra's community treasury pays **$38.15 million** to put its name on the Washington Nationals' stadium club.
4. Re-hook: everything now depends on one assumption — that people will always want Luna.
5. Explain the death spiral before it happens (the bad-math centrepiece): 1 UST redeems for $1 of Luna. At $80 a Luna that mints 0.0125 Luna. At 1 cent it mints 100. At a hundredth of a cent, 10,000. The lower Luna goes, the more Luna gets printed, which pushes it lower.

## Chapter 4 — Seven days in May (≈5:15–7:15)
Beats (day by day)
1. **May 7, 2022 (Sat):** large holders sell about **$285 million** of UST. UST dips to ~$0.985 on May 8.
2. **May 8–9:** LFG lends out **$1.5 billion** to defend the peg. Kwon tweets "Deploying more capital — steady lads" (⚠ exact text/date).
3. **May 9–10:** money runs out of Anchor. UST falls to the 60s, then (per Fortune) 35 cents.
4. The spiral runs exactly as the math says: holders redeem UST for Luna and dump it. Luna supply goes from about **350 million** coins to about **6.5 trillion** — roughly 18,500× — in days.
5. **May 12–13:** Luna, $119 a month earlier, trades below one-hundredth of a cent. UST: about 10 cents. The blockchain itself is halted on May 13.
6. Tally: about **$40 billion** gone (DOJ); Wikipedia: almost $45 billion in a week. Ordinary people had savings in the "safe 20% dollar".
7. Contagion: the crash helps topple crypto lenders and funds that summer (Three Arrows, Celsius, Voyager) — and, by November, FTX (callback to our Short; one line only).

## Chapter 5 — The run, the fake passport, the sentence (≈7:15–9:30)
Beats
1. Kwon says he isn't on the run. September 2022: South Korea issues an arrest warrant; Interpol red notice follows. Asked about his cancelled passport, he says he isn't using it anyway.
2. **March 23, 2023:** arrested at Podgorica airport, Montenegro, boarding a flight to Dubai on a **forged Costa Rican passport** (with forged Belgian papers in his bag). Four months in a Montenegrin jail for the passport.
3. A 21-month tug-of-war between South Korea and the US over who gets him. **December 31, 2024:** extradited to the United States.
4. Meanwhile, civil case: **April 2024**, a New York jury finds Kwon and Terraform liable for fraud; **June 2024**, a **$4.47 billion** settlement with the SEC (Kwon's share: about $204 million). Terraform is already bankrupt (filed January 2024).
5. **August 12, 2025:** he pleads guilty to two counts and admits he knew his statements were false.
6. **December 11, 2025:** Judge Paul Engelmayer gives him **15 years** — three more than prosecutors asked for — calling it "a fraud on an epic, generational scale". Forfeiture: about **$19 million**.
7. Close on the bad math: a "dollar" that was 13% backed, paying 20%, defended by printing 6.5 trillion of the thing that was supposed to defend it. Final line callback to the tweet.

## Who was convicted of what
- **Do Hyeong Kwon ("Do Kwon")** — pleaded guilty 2025-08-12 (SDNY) to conspiracy to commit commodities fraud, securities fraud and wire fraud, and to wire fraud. **15 years**, 2025-12-11; ~$19M forfeiture. Also: 4 months in Montenegro (forged passport, 2023). Civil: found liable by jury (SEC, April 2024); $4.47B settlement.
- **Daniel Shin** (co-founder) — charged in South Korea; **no conviction verified → "alleged"/avoid.**
- Han Chang-joon (ex-finance officer) — Montenegro passport sentence; extradited to Korea (Wikipedia) — skip.

- "He named his daughter Luna" — widely repeated; not checked.
- "Investors lost $60 billion" — $60B was the combined peak market value; court figure is $40B.
