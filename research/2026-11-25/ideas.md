## 2. Your 10-Leg Parlay Hands the Sportsbook 37%
- **Series / format:** What If · Myth vs Math
- **Folder:** `parlay-math/`
- **Killer number:** house edge 4.5% on one −110 bet → **37.2%** on a 10-leg parlay (pays 643-to-1 on a true 1-in-1,024 shot) — pure arithmetic ✔
- **Angle:** the payout looks huge, but every leg you add compounds the book's cut; the fair price for ten coin flips is 1,024×, they pay 643×.
- **Hook seeds:**
  1. "You're probably an idiot if you bet parlays. Here's the math."
  2. "If you bet a ten-leg parlay, you're tipping the sportsbook thirty-seven percent."
  3. "This is the dumbest bet on your phone, and it's the one they advertise most."
- **Why it should perform:** targets the exact audience's habit; gambling is the hottest exposé topic right now; number is checkable by anyone in comments (comment bait).
- **Inspired by:** "Exposing the Gambling Epidemic" (Coffeezilla, 9.3–9.9M, ~4.2× median) — outlierkit.com/channel/coffeezilla
- **Risk:** must state assumptions on screen (every leg a true 50/50 priced at −110, independent legs, standard multiplied payout). Real same-game parlays are priced differently (usually worse). Not advice — never say "bet singles instead". Roast the habit, not bettors. Gambling content can be age-restricted/limited-ads: keep it math, no sportsbook logos, no odds screenshots.

## 5. Your Bank's Overdraft Fee Is a 17,000% Loan
- **Series / format:** What If · Scale Swap
- **Folder:** `overdraft-17000-apr/`
- **Killer number:** $34 fee on a $24 overdraft repaid in 3 days = **≈17,000% APR** ✔ (CFPB's own example, 2014). A 700% payday loan looks cheap next to it.
- **Angle:** price the fee the way every other loan must be priced, and the "courtesy" overdraft becomes the most expensive loan most people ever take.
- **Hook seeds:**
  1. "You're probably an idiot if you have overdraft 'protection' turned on."
  2. "Your bank will lend you $24 for three days. The interest rate is seventeen thousand percent."
  3. "Loan sharks charge less than your checking account. Here's the math."
- **Why it should perform:** everyone has a bank account; the number sounds fake but is a government calculation; pairs with #4 (payday 700% vs bank 17,000%).
- **Inspired by:** OutlierKit-noted finance hook "You're probably losing $X a year doing this" — outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-finance-niche-creators/ ; Kullberg fine-print pattern
- **Risk:** it is an *APR-equivalent* of a flat fee, not a contractual rate (banks aren't required to disclose an APR for overdraft). Say "works out to". The CFPB median fee ($34) is from a 2014 study; many big banks have since cut or dropped overdraft fees — say "a typical $34–$35 fee". Roast the setting, not people who are broke.
