> **Gate (mandatory):** read `RULES.md` first. Write every script with `brand/skills/` per STYLE §22, run `brand/tools/script_gate.py` before voice (FAIL = rewrite), and check the VIDEO GATE in RULES.md before queuing. Do not send scripts to the owner.

Every day: **at least 8 unique Shorts topics — 8 posts per platform per day minimum** (user rule 2026-11-24; each topic = YouTube + TikTok + IG/FB cuts), plus **1 unique long-form on Monday, Wednesday and Friday** (US Eastern), built **one at a time**, and each one is **published automatically as soon as it passes QA** (standing user authorization, 2026-11-24: "don't ask for approval"). Order: Shorts 1–4 → (Mon/Wed/Fri) long-form → Shorts 5–8. Build ahead the night before so the 08:00–14:00 slots are already filled when the day starts. If a day is short of 8, add extra evening slots (e.g. 21:00, 23:00, 23:30) to catch up the same day — each queued and approved the moment it passes QA, so finished videos go out even if the day's usage runs out. The user can revoke this anytime ("stop auto-posting") — then go back to asking per post.

Re-read first: `CHANNEL.md`, `STYLE.md`, `brand/CHARACTER.md`, `brand/MEMES.md` (meme beats: 2–3 per Short, ~1 per 30–45 s long-form), `brand/sfx-library/manifest.json` (reusable CC0 sounds), `ideas.md`, and the newest long-form `videos/2026-11-25-impossible-number/DIRECTION.md` (template for long-form direction).

## Roles (user rule 2026-11-25 — model split)
- **Fable (researcher — Agent tool, model "fable"):** all research — **fact research AND content research**. Content research = map competitors in the niche (YouTube Shorts/long-form, TikTok, IG Reels), find their viral outliers (3–10× their normal views), extract what's working (topics, villain types, hook formulas, titles/thumbnails, length, pacing, CTAs, trends, gaps nobody covers), and write `research/<date>/competitors.md`; refresh it at least weekly (Sunday) and skim for new outliers daily. Ideas are marked "inspired by: <outlier>" — copy the PATTERN on a NEW topic, never a competitor's topic, script, footage or audio. Fact research: Finds and ranks topic ideas (outliers in the niche, court-proven cases, verifiable hypotheticals), checks ideas.md for repeats, pulls primary/secondary sources (DOJ/SEC/court records, Wikipedia, reputable press), builds the **number spine** (every figure with its source + date), flags anything unverifiable or still "alleged", and writes `research.md` in the video folder (facts, quotes allowed on screen, sources, risks). One Fable agent can research a whole day's batch in parallel.
- **Opus (creator + director, this session):** picks from Fable's ranked list, writes every hook (STYLE §14 gate) and the three platform scripts (§15), pronunciation pass (§16), direction/storyboards, thumbnail concepts, reviews every contact sheet and final, sends fixes, approves publishing. Opus does not do the research legwork and does not write animation code.
- **Sonnet agents (model "sonnet"):** animation engines, scenes, cuts, renders, mixes, thumbnails.
- **Haiku agents (model "haiku"):** narration + timings, sound sourcing, captions, upload packages, PostFast queueing chores.

## Uniqueness rules (non-negotiable — YouTube demonetizes repetitive/mass-produced content)
- Never reuse a topic, case, hypothetical, script, hook line or thumbnail layout already in `ideas.md` (check before choosing).
- Every video has its own story/insight, script, visuals and edit. Shared engine/character/brand are production tools, never the content.
- Rotate series and formats: Shorts alternate What If / Decoded; no two consecutive uploads use the same format (Counter, Scale Swap, Pitch vs Math, Case File, Dilemma/Two Buttons, Spot the Tell, Timeline, Myth vs Math).
- Long-form topics are bigger stories (a case or a hypothetical with 3–5 chapters), not stretched Shorts.
- Decoded = 100% accurate (STYLE §19): court-proven only; every fact primary-sourced or confirmed by 2+ independent sources, else cut; pending = "alleged". What If = anything goes as long as money is the theme (random/absurd premises welcome), but the math and real-world inputs must be correct and assumptions labeled on screen.
- Log every chosen idea in `ideas.md` (date, series, format, title, one-line angle, sources) at the moment it's picked.

## Daily pipeline
1. **Ideas (Fable):** pick 8 Short ideas + 1 long-form idea using research (Wikipedia + WebSearch; outliers in the niche). Hard-to-decide dilemmas and impossible-number frauds perform; vary them.

3. **Hook gate (Opus) — rule #1, before anything else:** follow STYLE.md §14. Write 10 outrageous hook variants ("This guy was so dumb…", "You're probably an idiot if…"), keep the strongest true one, design a frame-0 pattern break, and open a loop paid off at 75–80%. Mild edge only (no f-words/slurs in voice, title, thumbnail); never call an unconvicted person a fraud. A script that fails the gate is rewritten, not built.
4. **Scripts (Opus) — THREE per topic (STYLE.md §15), each built on the story loop (§18: stakes → big question → twist → rehooks) and humanized (§21: contractions, reactions, talk to the viewer, spoken rhythm):** a YouTube Shorts script, a TikTok script and an Instagram/Facebook Reels script, each with its own hook, length and CTA style (IG/FB = most vulgar/disrespectful, 15–25 s, built for sends + saves). Same facts, different cut. Voice each separately; build each edit separately (shared topic assets OK).
   Then, for the YouTube script:  weave like / share / follow into the story per STYLE.md §13 (spread out, one clause each, never a pitch). Shorts 70–115 words (28–45 s); long-form 1,350–1,600 words (≥ 8:30 so mid-rolls work later). Pattern-break hook in the first 2 s; re-hooks; payoff at 75–80%; loop line for Shorts. ## If something fails
- Usage limit / long build: ship the Shorts first; the long-form can slip to the next day — never ship an unreviewed video.
- PostFast account DISABLED: tell the user; don't schedule to it.
- Temp host blocked: tell the user; offer manual upload.

## Build ahead + analytics loop (user rule 2026-11-24)
- **Build ahead freely** when there's time/usage: research, scripts, voice, renders for future days are all fine.
- **Scheduling horizon = 7 days max.** Never schedule a post more than ~7 days out. Finished videos beyond that wait in `queue.md` (unscheduled) so they can still be improved or replaced after we see analytics.
- **Daily (start of every build session):** pull `get_post_analytics` for the last 7 days (all platforms) and append a dated block to `ANALYTICS.md`: per post — platform, title/hook, views, avg watch time / % viewed, likes, comments, shares, saves (IG saveRate, reelsSkipRate), TikTok full_video_watched_rate. Flag the top 20% and bottom 20%.
- **Weekly review (every Sunday ET, before building):** write `## Week of …` in `ANALYTICS.md`: which topics, series (Decoded/What If), formats, hook patterns, lengths, platforms and slots won/lost, with numbers. Then write **3–5 concrete changes** for next week (e.g. "more court-convicted villains", "IG cuts under 18 s", "drop the 08:00 slot"), and apply them: update STYLE.md/PRODUCTION.md if it's a rule change, re-rank ideas, and revise or replace any already-scheduled post in the next 7 days that the data says will underperform.
- **Replicate winners, never repeat them:** a winning video gets a *new* topic with the same ingredients (hook pattern, structure, length, villain type) — never the same topic or script (uniqueness rules still apply). Log "inspired by <winner>" in ideas.md.
- Small samples: don't overreact to < 500 views; judge patterns over ≥ 3 posts.

## PostFast YouTube cap (discovered 2026-11-25)
PostFast allows **max 10 YouTube posts per UTC day** (counted by scheduled time; error `platformRateLimit.youtube`). ET 20:00 and later = next UTC day. Standard day fits: UTC day D = ET(D−1) 20:00 + 22:00 (2) + ET(D) 08:00–18:00 Shorts (6) + 17:00 long-form (1) = 9. Before creating YouTube posts, `list_posts` (platform YOUTUBE) for that UTC day and count; if full, move the YouTube cut to the next ET slot at/after 20:00 (TikTok/IG/FB keep their slot). Extra evening catch-up slots (21:00/23:00/23:30 ET) eat the NEXT UTC day's YouTube budget — avoid them unless needed.
