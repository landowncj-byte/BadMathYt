## THE MISTAKE — the recurring beat (the show's signature)
Every case chapter has one line that starts "The mistake?" (P024, P042, P064, P085, P107). On that word:
1. hard cut to black for 4 frames, heartbeat/low drone from brand/sfx-library;
2. a red rubber stamp **THE MISTAKE** slams in (same design, size and position every time, top-left third, slight rotation, ink-bleed texture), stamp SFX;
3. the evidence object of that case drops onto a white evidence tray under a magnifier with an evidence tag "EXHIBIT A/B/C/D/E": Monopoly board + ketchup bottle with fingerprint lifts · half-eaten salami sandwich in an evidence bag · car with a tiny mic and sound waves · a jail-cell card with Specs' illustration and a speech bubble with no words ("…") · a hedge with a newspaper bundle;
4. the beat label (top-right) switches to THE MISTAKE for the length of that paragraph and the one after it.
A running **MISTAKE TRAY** (five empty slots, bottom-left, small) fills one slot per chapter, so the viewer sees the collection grow; it becomes the evidence board in ch.6.
Other beat labels: THE CASE · THE PLAN · THE HAUL · THE MISTAKE · THE CATCH · THE SENTENCE (ch.5 adds THE TWIST).
