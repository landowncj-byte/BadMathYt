## FACTS USED (Decoded — every line is ✔2 / primary-extract in research.md, attributed)
- Bottles of one Burgundy wine in his April 2008 New York consignment were labelled 1945–1971; the winemaker objected and the lots were withdrawn; his family began making that wine in 1982 (37 years after 1945) — ✔2 (trial testimony).
- 2006: his cellar sold for about $35M across two auctions, one a record ≈$24.7M — ✔2 (on screen: "≈$35M · 2 AUCTIONS · 2006").
- Arrested at home March 2012; prosecutors called the house a "wine factory"; prosecutors said older wines were mixed with newer ones and rebottled; thousands of labels — ✔2, voiced with "prosecutors".
- Collector testimony: $2.1M paid for 219 bottles he says were fake — ✔2, voiced as "says he paid" (no name voiced; never mocked).
- Convicted Dec 2013 (jury, SDNY) · 10 years · $28.4M restitution · $20M forfeiture — ✔2 (DOJ).
