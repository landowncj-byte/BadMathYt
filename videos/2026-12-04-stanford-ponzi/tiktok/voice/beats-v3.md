## Facts / math used
✔2 only: Texan R. Allen Stanford owned Stanford International Bank, an offshore bank on Antigua (Caribbean); it sold certificates of deposit (CDs = lock your money up, bank promises a fixed return); promised returns above normal banks (prosecutors: "inflated returns"); new customers' money paid old customers (Ponzi per DOJ/jury); more than $7 billion in CDs over about 20 years; Feb 2009 the SEC sued, alleging a "massive ongoing fraud"; 6 March 2012 Houston federal jury convicted him on 13 of 14 counts (fraud, conspiracy to commit money laundering, obstruction) after a ~6-week trial; 14 June 2012 sentenced to 110 years in prison (DOJ); prosecutors had asked for 230 years, the defense about 41 months; $5.9 billion money judgment. Everyday math (arithmetic): $7B ÷ 20 yrs = $350M a year; ÷ 365 = $958,904 a day ("almost a million dollars every single day").
