## Facts / math used
ASSUMES (on screen): median full-time weekly pay $1,232 (BLS usual weekly earnings, Q3 2026, released 21 Oct 2026, ✔1 — said "the government's labor stats"); × 52 = $64,064 a year; savings = a fixed % of gross pay, deposited once a year, no raises, no taxes on gains, no inflation; growth lines 7% and 4% a year are ASSUMPTIONS, not forecasts or advice. Math (research Python, re-checked): 15% = $9,610/yr (÷ 12 = $800.8 → "about eight hundred a month"); no growth: $1,000,000 ÷ 9,610 = 104.1 yrs; 104 ÷ 40-yr career = 2.6 careers; 10% → 156.1 yrs; 20% → 78.0 yrs; 100% of pay → 15.6 yrs ("fifteen and a half"); at 7%: n = ln(1 + 1e6·0.07/9,610)/ln(1.07) = 31.3 yrs; at 4%: 41.9 yrs; own deposits at 7% = 9,610 × 31.3 ≈ $300,800 ("about three hundred thousand") → growth ≈ $699,000 ("about seven hundred thousand").

## Cautions applied
No advice: never "invest in stocks / index funds"; 7% always said as "an assumption, not a promise"; no "you need a million to retire"; no moral about whether a median earner can or should save; the median is "the middle full-time worker", never "the average person". $1,232 said "twelve thirty-two"; $64,064 said "sixty-four thousand dollars" in voice (exact on screen).

## Story loop
STAKES: a million is everyone's goal. · QUESTION: how long does it take on a normal paycheck? · TWIST: 104 years if the money just sits — it only shrinks to ~31 because money earns money, and ~$700K of the million is growth. · REHOOKS: $64K a year → 15% = $800/month → 104 years → even 100% = 15.5 years → 7% assumption → 31 years → your money vs growth split.
