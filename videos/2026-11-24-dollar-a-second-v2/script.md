# What If You Earned $1 Every Second? (v2, 3D)
Same voiceover, words and timing as v1 (see ../2026-11-24-dollar-a-second/script.md for the number spine).
Built with the video-gen-skills plugin: `zach-d-films` (three.js 3D engine), `unscrollable` (retention rules), `youtube-automation` (package + QA).

## unscrollable 7-point check
| Rule | How v2 does it |
|---|---|
| One object + one target in frame 0 | A falling $1 bill + a giant empty glass tank labeled $1 BILLION |
| Stakes in ≤ 1 s | "One dollar." + the tank behind him |
| Slow, visible, continuous approach | The cash pile at his feet grows at true scale, and the tank fill + progress bar creep up |
| Escalation every 3–5 s | 1 s → minute → hour → day (sun crosses the sky) → $1M (he cheers on a 1 m cube) → years (day/night) |
| Payoff held to the end, silence before impact | Dip to black at 19.7–20.7 s, then the reveal |
| Physical, visual payoff | The tank slams full, and he's suddenly old: white hair, beard, stoop, jaw drop |
| Seamless loop | Old man and a full tank → frame 0: young man, first bill |

## Visual scale (honest)
- $1 bill ≈ 2.61 × 6.14 × 0.0043 in ≈ 1.13e-6 m³.
- $1M in $1 bills ≈ 1.13 m³ → shown as a ~1.04 m cube (stretched 1.35× wide for readability).
- $1B ≈ 1,130 m³ → the tank: 11 m wide × 11.9 m tall (π·5.5²·11.9 ≈ 1,130 m³).
- Falling bills are drawn ~1.6× real size so they read on a phone.
