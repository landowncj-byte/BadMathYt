## Number spine (all arithmetic, assumptions: no taxes, no interest, no spending)
- 1 min = 60 s → $60 · 1 h = 3,600 s → $3,600 · 1 day = 86,400 s → $86,400
- $1,000,000 ÷ $86,400/day = 11.57 days → "11.6 days" / "under twelve days"
- 1 year = 365 × 86,400 = 31,536,000 s → $31,536,000 (3.2% of $1B); 2 years = $63,072,000 (6.3%)
- $1,000,000,000 ÷ 86,400 = 11,574 days ÷ 365 = 31.71 years → "31.7 years" / "nearly thirty-two"
- 1B ÷ 1M = 1,000 (money) and 11,574 ÷ 11.574 = 1,000 (time) → "1,000× the money, 1,000× the time"
- On-screen progress bar = balance ÷ 1,000,000,000
