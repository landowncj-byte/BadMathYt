## ASSUMES (What If: on screen as a pinned chip from beat 2 to the end, plus the HYPOTHETICAL chip)
`ASSUMES: every US bill ≈ 1 g · 0.0043 in thick · crisp, flat, uncompressed · average US adult man = CDC measured averages (199.0 lb, 68.9 in, Aug 2021–Aug 2023) · whole notes only · HYPOTHETICAL, nobody is offering this`
Math (exactly as research.md): weight pile 90,264 × $20 = **$1,805,280** · height stack 16,023 × $100 = **$1,602,300** · gap **$202,980** · 1 lb ≈ 454 notes · 1 in ≈ 233 notes · hundreds stack weighs **35.3 lb** · break-even **2.56 lb per inch**. Spoken numbers are rounded; exact figures go on screen.
Not used on purpose: no body-shaming line, no BMI, no "exactly one gram", no women's figures voiced (on-screen footnote only if the director wants it: woman avg $1,558,540 vs $1,476,700).
