## ASSUMES + math (What If — all from research/2026-11-26/weight-vs-height-cash/research.md; nothing new)
`ASSUMES: every US bill ≈ 1 g · 0.0043 in thick · crisp, flat, uncompressed · average US adult man = CDC measured averages (199.0 lb, 68.9 in ≈ 5'9", Aug 2021–Aug 2023) · whole notes only · HYPOTHETICAL, nobody is offering this` — pinned chip + footnote on every cut.
- Weight pile: 199.0 lb × 453.59 g = 90,264 notes × $20 = **$1,805,280** (spoken "about one point eight million").
- Height stack: 68.9 in ÷ 0.0043 = 16,023 notes × $100 = **$1,602,300** (spoken "about one point six million").
- Gap: **$202,980** (spoken "two hundred grand").
- One pound of twenties: 453.59 notes × $20 = **$9,071.85** (spoken "about nine thousand / nine grand").
- One inch of hundreds: 232.56 notes × $100 = **$23,255.81** (spoken "about twenty-three thousand / twenty-three grand").
- Why it flips: an inch is worth 23,255.81 ÷ 9,071.85 = **2.56 lb** of twenties; the average man has 199.0 ÷ 68.9 = 2.89 lb per inch (more than 2.56) so the weight pile wins. "Way more pounds than inches": 199 vs 69.
- Flip rule (TikTok payoff): height in inches × 2.56 = break-even weight in lb (5'9" → 176.9 lb; on screen 5'4" → 164, 5'9" → 177, 6'0" → 185).
- Everyday comparison (our arithmetic): $202,980 ÷ $100 a day = 2,030 days = 5.56 years → "a hundred bucks a day for over five years" (YouTube).
- "Sixty-nine inches" = 68.9 rounded; exact 68.9 IN on screen.
Not used: BMI, any body-shaming line, "exactly one gram", women's figures (optional on-screen footnote only), 35-lb / 32-ft side facts.
