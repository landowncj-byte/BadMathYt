## Number spine (hypothetical; assumptions on screen)
- B: $1,000 × 52 = $52,000/yr. Passes A at $1,000,000 ÷ $52,000 = 19.2 years. 60 years × $52,000 = $3,120,000.
- A at 5%: $1,000,000 × 0.05 = $50,000/yr, principal kept → after 60 yrs A = $1M + $3M = $4.0M of value vs B's $3.12M.
- Inflation 3%: $1,000 ÷ 1.03^30 = $1,000 ÷ 2.427 = $412 → buying power down 58.8% ("-59%").
- Assumptions: 5% return and 3% inflation are illustrative round numbers, not predictions; no taxes. Not financial advice.
