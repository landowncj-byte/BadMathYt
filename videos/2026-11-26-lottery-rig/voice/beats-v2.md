## FACTS USED (Decoded — the conservative, attributed wording research.md gives)
- Eddie Tipton was information security director at the multi-state association whose computers draw the numbers — research beat 1 + every cited headline.
- June 2017 plea: he told the court he wrote code that let him predict winning numbers, gave those numbers to other people who won and shared the winnings with him — voiced as "he told a court / he said" (paraphrase, no quote marks).
- "Rigging lotteries in five states" = the wording research.md gives for the guilty plea.
