## Facts / math used
ASSUMES (on screen): one-way commute 26.4 min (Census Bureau ACS, 2022 national average, ✔1 — said "about twenty-six minutes… back in twenty twenty-two", never "the 2024 average"); round trip ×2; 250 workdays a year; 40 working years; no remote days; median wage $24.51/hr (BLS OEWS May 2025) for the value line only. Math (research Python, re-checked): 26.4 × 2 = 52.8 min a day ("almost an hour"); × 250 ÷ 60 = 220 h a year (= 5.5 forty-hour work weeks; ÷ 24 = 9.2 days nonstop, "about nine days"); × 40 = 8,800 h; ÷ 24 = 366.7 days ("a full year, nonstop, no sleep"); ÷ 2,080 = 4.2 years of full-time work; × $24.51 = $215,688 ("about two hundred fifteen thousand dollars" of time value, not money lost).

## Cautions applied
Research "don't say": no advice (no quit / move / go remote), no "commuting is wasted" — the twist is the scale, kept non-preachy ("some people love that drive"); 26.4 min is the 2022 figure, said as such; no gas/car costs; the $215K is the VALUE of the time at the middle wage, never "money you lose". 8,800 said "eight thousand eight hundred".
