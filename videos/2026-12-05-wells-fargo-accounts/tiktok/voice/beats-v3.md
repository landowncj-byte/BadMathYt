## Facts / math used
✔2: 2002–2016 unrealistic sales goals (DOJ statement of facts); employees opened accounts customers never asked for, used made-up PINs and forged signatures, and moved customers' money between accounts ("simulated funding") to hit numbers; the bank's own analysis: more than 2 million deposit and credit-card accounts that may not have been authorized (≈1.5M deposit + 565K credit card); Sept 2016 regulators fined it $185 million ($100M CFPB + $35M OCC + $50M Los Angeles); about 5,300 employees fired; Feb 2020 the bank agreed to pay $3 billion ($2.5B DOJ + $0.5B SEC) to resolve criminal and civil investigations, under a three-year deferred prosecution agreement, and admitted top managers knew about the practices; ex-head of community banking Carrie Tolstedt pleaded guilty (2023) to obstructing a bank examination, sentenced Sept 2023 to 3 years' probation incl. 6 months' home confinement, plus a $17M penalty to the OCC. ✔1 (allowed, attributed): public cross-sell figure of about six products per customer. Everyday math (arithmetic): 2,000,000 accounts ÷ 525,600 minutes a year = 3.8 years at one account a minute, nonstop ("almost four years").
